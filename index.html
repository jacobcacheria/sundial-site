why i made this

because social media stopped feeling social a long time ago.

i wanted to create a quiet, private space for all the people in my life, where i could share silly thoughts, songs, pictures, voice memos, and so much more. i built sundial because i wanted to connect with those i love more, and see that connection right on my home screen.

honestly, i was just tired. tired of spam calls, spam texts, and a flood of unwanted emails from literally everything. i hate subscriptions. i wanted an intentional space free from all the annoying shit technology has nowadays, where every app wants your phone number just to collect a pile of data on you and sell it. sundial will never sell your data, and it never even asks for your phone number or email. everything you send is encrypted, so not even i can read it lol.
