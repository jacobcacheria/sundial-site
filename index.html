Sundial — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026

Sundial is a small app for two people to share a daily note: little timestamped messages that sync between your two paired phones. This policy explains, in plain language, what Sundial collects, why, and your choices. We keep it minimal on purpose.

Who we are

Sundial is made by Jacob Cacheria ("we," "us"). You can reach us at sundialskeeper@yahoo.com.

What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

  • Sign in with Apple identifier. When you sign in, Apple gives us a unique ID for your account. We use it to recognize you and connect you to your shared notes. We do not receive or store your email address. Sign in with Apple keeps that private.
  • Your name and your partner's name. The names you type during setup, so each of you can see who wrote what.
  • Your notes ("rays"). The text you write and the time you wrote it. These are shared with the one partner you pair with.
  • Pairing information. A temporary invite code and a record of which two accounts are paired (your "Beam").

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, browsing activity, advertising identifiers, or analytics about how you use the app. We do not show ads. We do not sell your data, ever.

How your information is used

  • To sync your shared note between your phone and your partner's phone.
  • To show names next to each message.
  • To pair the two of you and keep your notes private to your Beam.

Where it's stored

Your account and notes are stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a cloud service run by Google. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and protected by security rules so that only you and your paired partner can read your notes. Sign-in is handled by Apple.

These providers process data on our behalf:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple (Apple's Privacy Policy).
  • Google Firebase — account and note storage (Firebase Privacy & Security).

Who can see your notes

Only you and the single partner you pair with. Notes are not visible to other users, and they are not shared with anyone outside the providers listed above (who only store the data so the app can function).

Keeping or deleting your data

  • You can leave a Beam at any time in the app, which disconnects you from your partner.
  • To delete your account and associated data, email us at sundialskeeper@yahoo.com and we'll remove it.

Children

Sundial is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Effective date" above and post the new version here.

Contact

Questions? Email sundialskeeper@yahoo.com.
